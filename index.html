About

Gagan Chahal is a nationally and internationally recognized Enterprise Data & Analytics Executive, currently serving as Vice President at Regions Bank — leading bank-wide analytics modernization, data governance, and AI strategy across regulated portfolios.

He is known for translating complex data challenges into measurable business outcomes — from building audit-ready lineage and governance frameworks to enabling board-level reporting across multi-billion-dollar portfolios. His expertise spans enterprise BI, data quality & governance, AI-enabled analytics, and large-scale fintech transformation.
