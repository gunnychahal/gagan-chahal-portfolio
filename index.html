About

Data leader.
Strategy architect.
Trusted executive partner.

Gagan Chahal is a nationally and internationally recognized Enterprise Data & Analytics Executive, currently serving as Vice President at Regions Bank, where he leads bank-wide analytics modernization and governance strategy across regulated portfolios.

His expertise spans enterprise BI platforms, data governance operating models, AI-enabled analytics, and large-scale fintech transformation. He is known for translating complex data challenges into measurable business outcomes — from building audit-ready control frameworks to enabling board-level reporting across multi-billion-dollar portfolios.

Beyond his corporate role, Gagan is a published author in CDO Magazine and The AI Journal, and an invited judge for NASA (Tech Jury), The Stevie Awards, Future Engineers Org, and JA City. He volunteers with United Way of Salt Lake, UNICEF, and Save Humans Foundation, and his financial literacy programs have benefited thousands in communities across Utah.
